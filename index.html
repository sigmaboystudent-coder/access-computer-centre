Syntax: =IF(C2>=33, "Pass", "Fail")

Syntax: =VLOOKUP(...)

Yeh kya kaam karta hai: Data table me vertical data search karne ke liye.

Syntax: =COUNTIF(Range, Criteria)

Yeh kya kaam karta hai: Condition ke mutabiq cells ko count karne ke liye.
